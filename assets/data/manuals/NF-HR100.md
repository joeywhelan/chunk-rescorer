## 1. Important Safety Instructions

### General Safety Warnings
WARNING: Read and understand all instructions before operating the NF-HR100 Retractable Hose Reel. Failure to follow all instructions listed below may result in serious personal injury or property damage. Save these instructions for future reference.

1. Keep the work area clean and well-lit. Cluttered or dark work areas invite accidents.
2. Maintain proper footing and balance at all times. This enables better control of the hose reel in unexpected situations.
3. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment. Always wear ANSI Z87.1 approved eye protection when installing, operating, or maintaining this product.
4. Do not allow children or untrained individuals to operate the hose reel. Keep bystanders at a safe distance while operating the system.
5. Inspect the unit for damaged, cracked, or worn parts before each use. Do not operate a damaged hose reel.

### Installation Safety
WARNING: Mount the bracket only to a structure capable of supporting the weight of the hose reel (approximately 35 lbs when filled with water) and the pulling force required to extend the hose. Use the appropriate hardware for the wall material (such as wood studs, brick, or masonry). Failure to properly mount the bracket can lead to the unit falling, causing severe personal injury or equipment damage.

### Hose Reel Operation Safety
WARNING: High tension spring. The retraction mechanism is under high spring tension. Do not attempt to open the main plastic housing of the hose reel. There are no user-serviceable parts inside. Opening the casing may release the spring violently, causing severe injury.

CAUTION: Sudden retraction hazard. Do not release the hose while retracting. Always hold the hose firmly and walk it back to the reel during the retraction process. Releasing a fully extended hose can cause the hose and the hose stopper to whip uncontrollably, resulting in severe personal injury or damage to surrounding property.

1. Do not exceed the maximum operating pressure of 150 PSI.
2. Only use water with this hose reel. Do not use to transmit chemicals, fuels, flammable liquids, or gases.
3. Turn off the water source and relieve all water pressure from the system by opening the spray nozzle before attempting to perform any service, adjustment, or storage of the hose reel.
4. Protect the hose from sharp objects, heat, direct exposure to chemicals, or abrasive surfaces to prevent ruptures.
5. Store the unit indoors during freezing temperatures. Drain all water from the hose prior to winter storage to prevent freezing and cracking of the internal fluid path.

## 3. Assembly and Setup

### Bracket Mounting
WARNING: Mount the steel swivel bracket only to a structure capable of supporting the full operating weight of 35.0 lbs and the pulling force of hose extension. Wear ANSI Z87.1 eye protection.

1. Locate a solid wood stud or masonry wall. Do not mount directly to drywall or plaster.
2. Place the steel swivel bracket against the mounting surface at a recommended height of 3 to 4 feet. Use the bracket as a template to mark the center of the dual vertical slot mounting pattern.
3. For wood studs, drill 1/4-inch pilot holes to a depth of 2.5 inches. For masonry, drill 3/8-inch pilot holes and insert the included wall anchors.
4. Secure the bracket tightly using the four provided lag bolts. Verify the bracket is plumb.

### Hose Reel Installation
CAUTION: Heavy object hazard. Use caution when lifting the 26.5 lbs empty hose reel to avoid personal injury.

1. Align the mounting shaft of the NF-HR100 main housing with the sleeve of the installed wall bracket.
2. Lower the main housing shaft directly into the bracket sleeve until it is completely seated.
3. Verify that the hose reel swivels smoothly through its entire 180-degree range of motion without contacting surrounding obstacles.

### Water Source Connection
1. Inspect the 3/4-inch GHT solid brass inlet fitting on the side of the reel housing. Ensure the internal rubber washer is correctly seated.
2. Thread the female end of the 6-foot lead-in hose onto the brass inlet fitting. Tighten securely by hand, then apply an additional quarter-turn with a wrench. Do not overtighten.
3. Connect the opposite end of the lead-in hose to your outdoor water spigot.
4. Turn on the spigot slowly to inspect connections for leaks before pressurizing the main unit.

## 4. Operation

### Extending and Locking the Hose
To operate the NF-HR100 Retractable Hose Reel, always wear ANSI Z87.1 approved eye protection and ensure the work area is clear of obstructions.
1. Turn on the water supply at the outdoor water spigot to pressurize the system. Check for any leaks at the brass inlet fitting and the lead-in hose.
2. Grasp the solid brass outlet fitting or the attached spray nozzle at the end of the primary hose.
3. Pull the primary hose outward from the main housing. The hose reel will swivel on the wall-mounted steel swivel bracket up to 180 degrees to align with your direction of travel. Pull at a steady, moderate pace.
4. To lock the hose at any length, stop pulling when you reach the desired length. The internal automatic locking mechanism will engage with a clicking sound. Release the tension slowly; the hose will hold its position.
WARNING: Do not pull the primary hose past the yellow or red warning markers near the end of the 100-foot primary hose. Pulling beyond this point can damage the internal retraction spring and void the warranty.

### Retracting the Hose
CAUTION: Sudden retraction hazard. Never let go of the hose during retraction. Always hold the primary hose and walk it back to the main housing at a controlled pace. Failure to do so can cause the hose to whip, leading to severe personal injury or property damage.
1. Close the outdoor water spigot completely.
2. Open the spray nozzle or accessory attached to the brass outlet fitting to relieve all remaining water pressure from the system.
3. Pull the hose outward approximately 6 to 12 inches to release the internal automatic locking mechanism.
4. Walk the hose back toward the main housing as the automatic slow-retraction mechanism pulls it in. Keep your hands free of the hose entry slot on the main housing to avoid pinching.
5. Allow the hose to wrap evenly onto the internal drum until the high-impact rubber hose stopper contacts the hose entry mouth.

### Adjusting the Hose Stopper
The high-impact rubber hose stopper controls how much primary hose remains exposed outside the main housing when fully retracted.
1. Relieve all water pressure from the system as described in "Retracting the Hose".
2. Pull the hose out to the desired stopper position and allow the locking mechanism to engage.
3. Loosen the two screws on the rubber hose stopper using a Phillips screwdriver.
4. Slide the hose stopper along the primary hose to its new position.
5. Tighten the two screws securely to clamp the stopper onto the hose. Do not overtighten, as this can compress and damage the 5/8-inch hose.

## 5. Maintenance and Storage

### Regular Cleaning and Inspection
To ensure optimal operation and extend the service life of the NF-HR100 retractable hose reel, perform regular maintenance. Always wear ANSI Z87.1 approved eye protection during all maintenance procedures.

1. Inspect the 100-foot primary hose weekly for wear, cuts, or abrasions. Replace the primary hose if any leaks or structural defects are observed.
2. Keep the primary hose clean. If the hose becomes dirty, wipe it with a damp cloth as it retracts. Debris entering the main housing can damage the internal tracking mechanism and retraction spring.
3. Check the brass inlet fitting and brass outlet fitting for mineral buildup or corrosion. Clean the threads with a wire brush if necessary.
4. Verify that the rubber washer inside the 6-foot lead-in hose is intact and pliable. Replace damaged washers immediately to prevent leaks.
5. Apply a light coat of silicone-based lubricant to the metal mounting shaft of the steel swivel bracket every six months to maintain smooth 180-degree rotation. Do not use petroleum-based lubricants, which can degrade the plastic housing.

### Winter Storage Preparation
CAUTION: Freezing hazard. Remaining water inside the hose reel will expand during freezing temperatures, causing catastrophic damage to the internal brass and polymer fluid path. This damage is not covered under warranty.

1. Shut off the water supply at the outdoor water spigot completely.
2. Open the spray nozzle or terminal accessory to relieve all water pressure from the system.
3. Disconnect the 6-foot lead-in hose from both the outdoor water spigot and the brass inlet fitting of the main housing. Drain all water from the lead-in hose.
4. Pull out the primary hose completely to drain all standing water from the internal drum. Walk the hose back slowly using the automatic slow-retraction mechanism.
5. Lift the 26.5 lbs empty hose reel straight up off the wall-mounted steel swivel bracket. Do not attempt to lift the unit when full of water.
6. Store the hose reel and lead-in hose in a dry, climate-controlled indoor environment, such as a heated garage or basement, where temperatures remain consistently above freezing (32°F / 0°C).

## 6. Troubleshooting

### Hose Retraction and Locking

Problem: Hose does not retract.
Possible cause: Locking mechanism is engaged.
Corrective action: Pull the primary hose outward 6 to 12 inches to release the lock, then walk it back.

Problem: Hose does not lock.
Possible cause: Pulling speed is too fast.
Corrective action: Pull the primary hose at a steady, moderate pace and stop slowly to allow locking.

Problem: Hose retracts slowly.
Possible cause: Hose is dirty or covered in debris.
Corrective action: Fully extend the primary hose and wipe it with a damp cloth before walking it back.

### Leaks and Connections

Problem: Water leaking at the housing connection.
Possible cause: Loose connection; damaged rubber washer inside the lead-in hose.
Corrective action: Inspect the brass inlet fitting, verify the rubber washer is correctly seated, and tighten the connection.

Problem: Water leaking at the spray nozzle or accessory connection.
Possible cause: Loose nozzle; thread damage.
Corrective action: Tighten the nozzle onto the 3/4-inch GHT brass outlet fitting.

Problem: Water pressure from primary hose is low.
Possible cause: Water spigot not fully open; kink in lead-in hose or primary hose.
Corrective action: Fully open the outdoor water spigot, inspect both hoses, and remove any kinks.

### Swivel and Mounting

Problem: Reel housing is difficult to rotate or does not swivel smoothly.
Possible cause: Mounting shaft lacks lubrication.
Corrective action: Apply silicone-based lubricant to the metal mounting shaft of the steel swivel bracket.
