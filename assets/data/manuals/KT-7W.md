## 1. Important Safety Instructions

Read all instructions thoroughly before beginning the installation or operation of the Kestrel Home Systems KT-7W 7-Day Programmable Wi-Fi Thermostat. Failure to follow these safety instructions can result in property damage, severe personal injury, or death. Retain this manual in a secure and accessible location for future reference.

### Code Compliance and Industry Standards
This device must be installed in strict accordance with all applicable local, state, and national building, electrical, and mechanical codes.
1. In the United States, all wiring and installation procedures must conform to the National Electrical Code (NEC), NFPA 70, and any relevant local ordinances.
2. In Canada, installations must comply with the Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) Part I, CSA C22.1, and provincial or local regulations.
3. This thermostat is certified to meet UL 60730-1 and UL 60730-2-9 standards for automatic electrical controls for household and similar use.
4. All heating and cooling equipment managed by this thermostat must comply with the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) standards, specifically NFPA 54 (National Fuel Gas Code) and NFPA 90A/90B (Standard for the Installation of Air-Conditioning and Ventilating Systems).

### Qualified Installer Requirements
WARNING: Risk of electrical shock, fire, or equipment damage. This thermostat should only be installed, configured, or serviced by a licensed, qualified HVAC technician or a registered electrical contractor. An unqualified person attempting to install or service this equipment assumes all risks of improper wiring, system malfunction, and electrical hazards.
1. Verify that the power supply to all connected heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems is completely disconnected at the main service panel before attempting installation or maintenance.
2. Ensure that the installer possesses the necessary diagnostic tools, including a calibrated digital multimeter, to verify low-voltage circuits.
3. Confirm that the systems to be controlled do not exceed the electrical ratings specified in Section 2 of this manual.

### Electrical Hazards and High Voltage Warnings
WARNING: High voltage hazard. This thermostat is a low-voltage (24 VAC) device. Do not install this thermostat on line-voltage systems, such as 120-volt or 240-volt electric baseboard heaters, radiant ceiling heating systems, or line-voltage HVAC units. Connecting this low-voltage thermostat to line voltage will destroy the control circuitry, create a severe fire hazard, and present a risk of lethal electrical shock.
CAUTION: Equipment damage hazard. To prevent damage to the compressor in your cooling or heat pump system, do not operate the cooling system when the outdoor temperature is below 50°F (10°C).
1. If you are replacing a line-voltage thermostat, a licensed electrician must install a step-down transformer and low-voltage relay to safely interface the KT-7W with the heating system.
2. Prior to handling any wiring, use a non-contact voltage tester to confirm that no high-voltage lines are present in the wall cavity.
3. Insulate any unused wire leads with electrical tape or wire nuts to prevent accidental short circuits that could damage the thermostat's internal processor or the HVAC system's control board.

### Gas Leak and Fire Safety Precautions
WARNING: Explosion and asphyxiation hazard. If you smell gas, detect a suspected gas leak, or hear gas escaping from your heating appliance, follow these instructions immediately:
1. Do not touch any electrical switches, including the thermostat touchscreen or light switches.
2. Do not use any phone, cellular device, or wireless appliance in the building.
3. Evacuate all occupants from the premises immediately.
4. From a safe location outside the building, call your gas supplier or local fire department.
5. Do not re-enter the building until qualified service personnel or emergency responders have declared the area safe.

### Battery Safety and Environmental Disposal
CAUTION: Chemical burn and environmental hazard. When utilizing battery power for the KT-7W, use only high-quality AA alkaline batteries.
1. Do not use rechargeable or lithium-type batteries, as they may cause inaccurate voltage readings and improper system operation.
2. Install batteries with the correct polarity as indicated in the battery compartment.
3. Replace batteries immediately when the low-battery indicator appears on the touchscreen.
4. Dispose of depleted batteries in compliance with local environmental laws and recycling ordinances. Never incinerate batteries.

### Compressor Protection and Short-Cycling
CAUTION: Compressor damage hazard. To prevent permanent damage to the compressor in your air conditioner or heat pump, the KT-7W incorporates an automatic compressor protection delay (anti-short-cycle timer). This feature delays compressor start-up for five minutes after a system shutdown. Do not bypass this delay during testing or initial startup unless authorized by a qualified technician.

## 3. Installation and Setup

### Location Selection and Wallplate Mounting
To ensure proper performance and accurate temperature sensing, the KT-7W must be installed in a location that conforms to standard building practices and code requirements, such as NFPA 90A. Install the thermostat approximately 5 feet (1.5 meters) above the finished floor in an area with good air circulation and constant temperature.

CAUTION: Equipment malfunction hazard. Do not install the thermostat on an exterior wall, behind a door, in a corner, near active heating or cooling registers, or in direct sunlight. Avoid placing the device close to concealed pipes, chimneys, or drafty areas.

1. Turn off all electrical power to the heating and cooling equipment at the main circuit breaker panel. Verify that the power is off using a digital multimeter on the low-voltage terminal board.
2. Remove the decorative cover plate from the thermostat base by pulling gently on the lower edge of the outer housing.
3. Align the mounting base on the wall and mark the locations of the mounting holes through the baseplate.
4. Drill 3/16-inch holes at the marked positions and press the provided plastic wall anchors firmly into the drywall until they are flush with the surface.
5. Feed the system wires through the wire opening in the center of the mounting plate.
6. Secure the base to the wall using the supplied mounting screws, ensuring that the base plate is perfectly level to prevent liquid crystal display distortion and ensure proper switch orientation.

### Wiring and Terminal Connections
All system wiring must comply with the National Electrical Code (NEC) NFPA 70 and local electrical codes. Ensure that all wires are stripped of insulation to exactly 3/8 inch (10 mm) before insertion into the terminal block.

WARNING: Electrical shock hazard. Disconnect all power before connecting or modifying any low-voltage wiring. Failure to do so may result in severe shock, shorted wires, or permanent damage to the integrated circuits in the thermostat and the HVAC control board.

Terminal Connections:
R: 24 VAC Power (Heating transformer)
RC: 24 VAC Power (Cooling transformer)
C: 24 VAC Common wire (Required for continuous Wi-Fi operation)
W: First-stage heating control (Conventional systems)
W2: Second-stage heating control or Auxiliary heat (Heat pump systems)
Y: First-stage compressor control (Cooling or Heat pump)
Y2: Second-stage compressor control
G: Fan control relay
O/B: Changeover valve for heat pump systems (Configure active state in setup)

Note: If a single transformer system is utilized, leave the factory-installed jumper wire in place between terminals R and RC. For dual-transformer systems, remove this metal jumper wire before connecting wires to R and RC.

### System Configuration and Power Setup
The KT-7W is a dual-power device capable of operating on either 24 VAC system power via the C-wire, or on battery power using two AA alkaline batteries. However, for continuous Wi-Fi connectivity and reliable app-based remote control, a dedicated C-wire connection is highly recommended.

1. If using battery power, slide the battery door open and install two new AA alkaline batteries, paying careful attention to the polarity markings embossed inside the compartment.
2. Align the outer touchscreen console with the mounting pins on the wallplate.
3. Push the thermostat housing straight onto the wallplate until it clicks into place.
4. Reapply electrical power to the HVAC system at the main breaker panel.
5. The digital touchscreen will illuminate and display the initial setup wizard. Follow the on-screen prompts to configure the system type (Conventional or Heat Pump), active O/B valve state, and fan operational mode (Gas/Electric) to match your HVAC equipment configuration.

## 4. Operation

### Touchscreen Interface and Display Layout
The KT-7W features an interactive resistive touchscreen console that provides comprehensive monitoring and precise adjustment of your indoor climate system. The home screen layout is divided into highly visible diagnostic and operational zones. The center of the screen permanently displays the current ambient indoor temperature. The active temperature setpoint is shown in the lower-right corner, directly adjacent to the manual adjustment arrows. The top status banner indicates the current day of the week, active system time, and Wi-Fi signal strength. Touch-sensitive buttons for primary menu options are aligned along the lower margin of the screen.
To interact with the resistive interface, use the soft pad of your fingertip to apply firm, direct pressure. Avoid using ballpoint pens, screwdrivers, or other sharp physical tools, which can puncture the protective polymer display layer. The touchscreen backlight is programmed to illuminate automatically upon any touch. When the KT-7W is wired with a continuous 24 VAC common wire (C-wire) as described in Section 3. Installation and Setup, the backlight remains illuminated for 30 seconds following the last interaction. However, if operating solely on battery power, the backlight duration is automatically restricted to 10 seconds to maximize the operational lifespan of the dual AA alkaline cells.

### System Mode and Fan Mode Selection
The thermostat's operational settings are controlled via separate, dedicated SYSTEM and FAN toggle menus on the primary display.
1. Touch the SYSTEM button in the lower-left corner to cycle through the available HVAC modes:
   - HEAT: The thermostat controls only your heating equipment. If configured for a heat pump system, the auxiliary heat strips will automatically engage if the room temperature drops more than 2°F (1°C) below the desired setpoint.
   - COOL: The thermostat controls only your cooling equipment.
   - OFF: Both heating and cooling are completely disabled.
   - AUX: Restricted to heat pump systems. This setting locks out the compressor and relies solely on auxiliary electric resistance heat or gas backup. Use this during compressor failure or extreme cold as noted in Section 1. Important Safety Instructions.
2. Touch the FAN button to cycle through the continuous or demand-based air circulation modes:
   - AUTO: The indoor blower fan cycles on and off in direct synchronization with the heating or cooling equipment.
   - ON: The indoor blower fan runs continuously, providing uninterrupted air filtration even when the heating or cooling systems are idle.

CAUTION: Equipment damage hazard. To prevent permanent compressor damage, do not select the COOL mode if the outdoor temperature is below 50°F (10°C) as detailed in Section 1. Important Safety Instructions.

### Setting the 7-Day Program Schedule
The KT-7W allows you to program unique heating and cooling schedules for each day of the week. Each daily schedule consists of four distinct periods: Wake, Leave, Return, and Sleep.
To program your weekly schedule, execute the following procedure:
1. Press the MENU button, select SCHEDULE on the touchscreen, and press SELECT.
2. Choose the specific day you wish to program (e.g., MONDAY) using the on-screen arrow keys, then press EDIT.
3. The WAKE period indicator will flash. Use the UP and DOWN arrow keys to select the desired start time for this period, then press NEXT.
4. Adjust the target heating setpoint for the WAKE period using the arrow keys, then press NEXT.
5. Adjust the target cooling setpoint for the WAKE period, then press NEXT.
6. Repeat this sequence to program start times and temperature setpoints for the LEAVE, RETURN, and SLEEP periods.
7. After configuring all four periods for the selected day, press SAVE to commit the changes to non-volatile memory.
8. Select the COPY TO option to duplicate the completed schedule to other days of the week, or press NEXT to program remaining days individually.
9. Press RUN SCHEDULE to exit the menu and begin running the program.

### Temperature Holds and Program Overrides
You can manually override the active program schedule at any time to accommodate temporary changes in your routine. The KT-7W features two override methods:
1. Temporary Hold: Touch the UP or DOWN arrow keys to adjust the temperature. The display will show "TEMPORARY HOLD" and indicate the expiration time (the start of the next scheduled period). The schedule resumes automatically when this time is reached. Press RUN SCHEDULE to cancel immediately.
2. Permanent Hold: Adjust the temperature using the arrows, then press HOLD. The screen will display "PERMANENT HOLD." The thermostat maintains this setpoint indefinitely, ignoring the programmed schedule. To release this override, press the RUN SCHEDULE button.

### Wi-Fi Connectivity and App Pairing
Connecting your KT-7W to a 2.4 GHz wireless network enables remote control and programming via the Kestrel Home mobile application.
WARNING: Wi-Fi operational restriction. If the thermostat is operating exclusively on AA battery power, Wi-Fi communication is automatically deactivated to prevent rapid battery failure. A continuous 24 VAC common wire (C-wire) connection is required for all wireless operations.
1. Ensure your smartphone is connected to a 2.4 GHz network. The thermostat's internal radio is not compatible with 5 GHz wireless bands.
2. Touch the MENU button on the thermostat console, select the WI-FI submenu, and press CONNECT.
3. The thermostat will display "PAIRING" and broadcast a temporary local network SSID labeled "Kestrel_KT7W_XXXX".
4. Open the Kestrel Home app on your smartphone, select "Add New Device", and follow the prompts to connect your phone directly to the thermostat's pairing network.
5. Once connected, use the app to select your permanent home wireless network from the available list.
6. Input your home Wi-Fi security password. The Wi-Fi icon on the thermostat screen will change from flashing to solid when the connection is successful.

### Filter Change Reset and Maintenance Reminders
To maintain system efficiency and protect HVAC blower motors from premature failure, the KT-7W incorporates an integrated run-time monitor that alerts you when your system's air filter requires replacement.
1. When the cumulative fan runtime reaches your designated alert interval (factory default is 500 hours), "REPLACE FILTER" will flash on the touchscreen.
2. Locate your heating or cooling system's main return air plenum and replace the air filter with a new filter of matching dimensions and MERV rating.
3. To reset the run-time counter, press MENU, select FILTER MONITOR, and press and hold the RESET button on the touchscreen for 3 seconds. The run-time counter resets, and the flashing alert will clear.

## 5. Maintenance and Storage

### Touchscreen Cleaning and Display Care
To ensure the long-term responsiveness of the resistive touchscreen console and prevent physical degradation of the liquid crystal display (LCD), the interface must be cleaned with care. The outer screen is composed of a sensitive multi-layer polymer that can be easily scratched or chemically clouded.
1. Turn off the HVAC system at the main breaker panel or switch the SYSTEM mode to OFF to prevent accidental commands during cleaning.
2. Use only a dry, clean, lint-free microfiber cloth to gently wipe dust and fingerprints from the display surface. Apply light, even pressure.
3. For stubborn residues, lightly dampen the microfiber cloth with water or a diluted isopropyl alcohol solution (70% concentration or lower).
WARNING: Chemical exposure hazard. Do not spray cleaners or solvents directly onto the touchscreen. Liquids can seep through the bezel, causing short circuits in the internal printed circuit board (PCB) and creating a fire risk.
4. Allow the display to air dry completely before restoring power or cycling system modes.

### Battery Replacement and Power Management
As established in Section 3, the KT-7W can operate on dual-power or battery-only configurations. When powered primarily by batteries, or when utilizing batteries as a backup power supply, routine replacement of these cells is essential to prevent system failure.
1. A flashing low-battery icon will appear on the status banner when battery voltage drops below 2.6 VDC. Replace batteries within 30 days of this warning.
2. Slide the main thermostat housing straight off the wallplate by pulling outward from the lower edge. Do not twist the unit.
3. Remove the two depleted AA batteries from the battery compartment on the back of the console.
CAUTION: Corrosion hazard. Always replace both batteries simultaneously. Do not mix old and new batteries, or combine different brands. Use only high-quality AA alkaline batteries. Do not use rechargeable NiMH or lithium batteries, as they can cause erratic voltage readings or chemical leakage.
4. Insert two fresh AA alkaline batteries into the compartment. Match the positive (+) and negative (-) terminals precisely with the embossed polarity markings.
5. Align the housing back onto the wallplate and push firmly until the unit clicks into place.
6. Verify that the screen illuminates and the time displays correctly. Programmed schedules and Wi-Fi configurations are stored in non-volatile memory and remain intact during replacement.

### Sensor Verification and Calibration
To maintain temperature sensitivity, the internal thermistor should be inspected annually. Dust buildup inside ventilation slots can insulate the sensor, leading to inaccurate readings.
1. Locate the ventilation slots along the top and bottom edges of the thermostat housing.
2. Use a soft-bristled brush or clean, compressed air to clear dust or lint from these slots. Do not insert any tools into these openings.
3. To verify accuracy, place a calibrated digital thermometer next to the thermostat, approximately one inch away, and wait 15 minutes to stabilize.
4. If the displayed temperature differs from the thermometer by more than ±1°F (±0.5°C), access the installer setup menu to apply a calibration offset.
5. Press MENU, hold the select button for 5 seconds to access advanced installer options, navigate to CALIBRATION, and use the arrow keys to adjust the offset (up to ±3°F or ±1.5°C). Press SAVE.

### Seasonal Maintenance and Extended Absence
If the property will be vacant for an extended period, or if HVAC systems are shut down for seasonal transitions, specific storage procedures must be followed.
1. If the building is located in a region prone to freezing winter temperatures, do not switch the thermostat to OFF. Maintain a minimum heating setpoint of 50°F (10°C) to prevent pipes from freezing.
2. For homes undergoing absolute winterization where the main HVAC system is fully decommissioned, remove the KT-7W from its wallplate.
3. Extract the AA batteries from the battery compartment to prevent chemical leakage and terminal corrosion during prolonged storage.
4. Store the thermostat console in a clean, dry, climate-controlled environment with an ambient temperature between 40°F and 100°F (4°C and 38°C) and relative humidity below 90% non-condensing.
5. When returning the unit to service, install fresh AA batteries, snap the console back onto the wallplate, and verify system operation as outlined in Section 3.

## 6. Troubleshooting

### Power and Display Diagnostics

Problem: Touchscreen is blank and does not illuminate upon physical contact.
Possible cause: Blown fuse or tripped circuit breaker at the main electrical panel; depleted AA batteries; loose or incorrect connection at R, RC, or C terminals.
Corrective action: Verify that the circuit breaker at the main service panel is switched on; check and replace the two AA batteries in the compartment with fresh AA alkaline cells; remove the thermostat from the wallplate and inspect the wiring connections at the R, RC, and C terminals using a digital multimeter.

Problem: Low-battery icon is flashing on the status banner.
Possible cause: AA battery voltage has dropped below 2.6 VDC.
Corrective action: Pull the main thermostat housing off the wallplate and replace both AA batteries with new AA alkaline batteries as described in Section 5.

Problem: Touchscreen does not register touch inputs or behaves erratically.
Possible cause: Dirt or moisture on the resistive screen layer; loose mounting of the console to the wallplate.
Corrective action: Clean the touchscreen with a dry microfiber cloth as directed in Section 5; firmly push the console housing straight onto the wallplate until it clicks to ensure full connection with the mounting pins.

Problem: Backlight turns off after 10 seconds despite continuous interaction.
Possible cause: Thermostat is operating solely on battery power without a 24 VAC common wire (C-wire) connection.
Corrective action: Install a dedicated C-wire from the HVAC transformer to the C terminal on the wallplate to enable continuous 30-second backlight illumination.

Problem: Thermostat screen displays error code "E1" or "E2".
Possible cause: Internal temperature sensor (thermistor) failure or short circuit.
Corrective action: Clean the ventilation slots using compressed air; if the error code persists after resetting power at the main breaker, contact a qualified Kestrel Home Systems service technician.

### HVAC and System Operation

Problem: Heating system does not engage when target temperature is set above room temperature.
Possible cause: Compressor protection delay is active; SYSTEM mode is set to OFF; loose wiring at W or W2 terminals.
Corrective action: Wait 5 minutes for the automatic anti-short-cycle timer to expire; press the SYSTEM button to select HEAT mode; remove the housing and verify that the heating wire is securely connected to the W terminal.

Problem: Cooling system does not engage when target temperature is set below room temperature.
Possible cause: Outdoor temperature is below 50°F (10°C); SYSTEM mode is not set to COOL; loose wiring at Y or Y2 terminals.
Corrective action: Avoid operating cooling below 50°F (10°C) as noted in Section 1; press the SYSTEM button to select COOL; verify the cooling wire is securely connected to the Y terminal.

Problem: Indoor blower fan runs continuously when heating or cooling is idle.
Possible cause: FAN mode is manually set to ON instead of AUTO.
Corrective action: Press the FAN button on the home screen to change the fan operational mode from ON to AUTO.

Problem: Heat pump blows cold air in heating mode or warm air in cooling mode.
Possible cause: O/B changeover valve is misconfigured in the installer setup.
Corrective action: Access the advanced installer setup menu, select O/B VALVE, and toggle the setting to match your specific heat pump manufacturer's reversing valve requirements.

Problem: Auxiliary heating does not activate during extreme cold.
Possible cause: Incorrect wiring at the W2 terminal; SYSTEM mode is not configured for a 2H/2C heat pump system.
Corrective action: Access the setup wizard to verify the system type is set to Heat Pump; ensure the auxiliary heat wire is secured to the W2 terminal.

Problem: HVAC system cycles on and off too frequently (short-cycling).
Possible cause: Air filter is dirty, restricting airflow; thermostat is located in a drafty area or near a supply vent.
Corrective action: Replace the system air filter and reset the filter monitor; confirm the thermostat location conforms to the guidelines in Section 3.

### Wi-Fi and App Connectivity

Problem: Wi-Fi icon is flashing on the screen and wireless control is unavailable.
Possible cause: Thermostat is operating on battery power only; local network signal is weak or offline.
Corrective action: Verify that a 24 VAC C-wire is connected to the C terminal, as Wi-Fi is disabled on battery power; check the status of your home wireless router.

Problem: Thermostat cannot connect to the home wireless network.
Possible cause: Wireless router is transmitting on a 5 GHz band only; network security password was entered incorrectly.
Corrective action: Configure your router to enable a 2.4 GHz band (IEEE 802.11b/g/n); re-enter the network password in the Kestrel Home mobile application.

Problem: The Kestrel Home mobile app cannot find the thermostat during setup.
Possible cause: "Kestrel_KT7W_XXXX" pairing network is not active or has timed out.
Corrective action: Press MENU, select WI-FI, and press CONNECT to broadcast the temporary pairing network; ensure your smartphone is connected to this network.

Problem: App settings do not update on the physical thermostat console.
Possible cause: Firewall or router settings are blocking outgoing connection ports.
Corrective action: Configure your router firewall to allow outbound connections on standard HTTPS ports to the Kestrel Home Systems cloud server.
