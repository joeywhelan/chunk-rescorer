## 1. Important Safety Instructions

### General Safety Warnings
WARNING: Read and understand all instructions before operating the NF-D20B 20V Brushless 1/2-inch Drill/Driver. Failure to follow all instructions listed below may result in serious personal injury or property damage. Save these instructions for future reference.

1. Keep the work area clean and well-lit. Cluttered or dark work areas invite accidents.
2. Maintain proper footing and balance at all times. This enables better control of the drill/driver in unexpected situations.
3. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment. Always wear ANSI Z87.1 approved eye protection when installing, operating, or maintaining this product.
4. Do not allow children or untrained individuals to operate the drill/driver. Keep bystanders at a safe distance while operating the system.
5. Inspect the unit for damaged, cracked, or worn parts before each use. Do not operate a damaged drill/driver.
6. Stay alert, watch what you are doing, and use common sense when operating a power tool. Do not use a power tool while tired or under the influence of drugs, alcohol, or medication.
7. Prevent unintentional starting. Ensure the trigger switch is in the off-position and the forward/reverse selector is locked before connecting to a battery pack, picking up, or carrying the tool.
8. Remove any adjusting key or wrench before turning the power tool on. A wrench left attached to a rotating part of the tool may result in personal injury.

### Installation Safety
WARNING: Mount the charger bracket only to a structure capable of supporting the combined weight of the charger and battery pack (approximately 3 lbs) and the insertion force required to seat the battery. Use the appropriate hardware for the wall material (such as wood studs, brick, or masonry). Failure to properly mount the bracket can lead to the unit falling, causing severe personal injury or equipment damage.

1. Do not install the charger in direct sunlight, near heat sources, or in damp or wet environments. Keep the charger away from water and liquids at all times.
2. Ensure the mounting area is free from hidden utility lines, such as electrical wiring, gas pipes, or water plumbing, before drilling mounting holes.
3. Place the charger on a flat, non-flammable surface or mount it vertically on a wall. Do not cover the ventilation slots on the charger housing during operation.
4. When installing accessories, such as the belt hook, ensure they are securely fastened on the correct side of the drill/driver housing using the provided screws.

### Drill/Driver Operation Safety
WARNING: High speed rotating spindle. The brushless motor is under high torque tension. Do not attempt to open the main plastic housing of the drill/driver or the battery pack. There are no user-serviceable parts inside. Opening the casing may release internal components violently or cause electrical shock, causing severe injury.

CAUTION: Sudden bind-up hazard. Do not release the tool handle while drilling. Always hold the drill/driver firmly with both hands and resist torque reactions during the drilling process. Releasing a binding power tool can cause the tool housing to rotate uncontrollably, resulting in severe personal injury or damage to surrounding property.

1. Do not exceed the maximum rating or capacity of the 1/2-inch ratcheting chuck. Always ensure the drill bit or driving bit is fully inserted and locked.
2. Only use approved 2.0 Ah battery packs with this drill/driver. Do not use other battery packs, and do not attempt to transmit unapproved electrical currents.
3. Remove the battery pack and relieve all electrical current from the system by releasing the trigger before attempting to perform any service, adjustment, or storage of the tool.
4. Protect the battery pack contacts from sharp metal objects, heat, direct exposure to chemicals, or abrasive surfaces to prevent short circuits or cell ruptures.
5. Store the battery pack and charger indoors during freezing temperatures. Drain all remaining power charge down to approximately 50 percent prior to long-term storage to prevent cell degradation.
6. Always wear hearing protection when using the drill/driver for extended periods, as noise levels can exceed safe limits in enclosed spaces.

## 3. Assembly and Setup

### Charging the Battery Pack
WARNING: Fire and electrical shock hazard. Charge only Northfield Tools model NF-B202 lithium-ion battery packs in the model NF-C20 charger. Charging other battery types may cause them to burst, resulting in personal injury or property damage. Keep the charger and battery pack away from liquids and moisture.

1. Connect the charger (model NF-C20) to a standard 120V AC, 60 Hz electrical outlet. The charger LED indicator will illuminate solid green, indicating standby status.
2. Align the raised ribs on the battery pack (model NF-B202) with the guide slots on the charger housing.
3. Slide the battery pack firmly into the charger until it seats completely.
4. Observe the LED indicator. A solid red light indicates active charging. A solid green light indicates the battery pack is fully charged.
5. Slide the battery pack out of the charger and disconnect the charger from the power source.

### Installing and Removing the Battery Pack
WARNING: Accidental start-up hazard. Always verify that the forward/reverse selector is locked in the center off-position before installing or removing the battery pack.

1. Align the raised rib on the NF-B202 battery pack with the matching groove inside the base of the drill/driver handle.
2. Slide the battery pack forward into the tool handle until it clicks securely into place and the latch engages.
3. To remove the battery pack, press the battery release button and slide the battery pack backward, out of the handle.

### Installing the Belt Hook
WARNING: Falling tool hazard. Ensure the belt hook is fastened tightly to prevent the drill/driver from dropping. An unsecured tool can fall, causing personal injury.

1. Locate the mounting slots and threaded insert on either side of the drill/driver base.
2. Position the belt hook on the preferred side of the tool housing to support left- or right-handed use.
3. Insert the provided belt hook screw through the belt hook and into the threaded insert.
4. Tighten the screw securely using a manual phillips screwdriver. Do not over-tighten, as this can strip the plastic housing threads.

### Inserting and Removing Bits
WARNING: Pinch hazard. Do not hold the outer sleeve of the 1/2-inch ratcheting chuck with one hand while initiating motor rotation with the trigger switch to tighten or loosen bits. This can cause severe hand friction or skin pinch injuries.

1. Lock the forward/reverse selector in the center position and remove the battery pack.
2. Rotate the single-sleeve ratcheting chuck counterclockwise to open the chuck jaws wide enough for the bit shank.
3. Clean any dust or debris from the bit shank before insertion.
4. Insert the bit straight into the chuck jaws, ensuring it is centered.
5. Rotate the single-sleeve ratcheting chuck clockwise by hand to close the jaws.
6. Once the jaws grip the bit shank, continue rotating the chuck sleeve clockwise until you hear distinct ratcheting clicks.
7. Pull on the bit to verify it is securely locked in place.
8. To remove the bit, rotate the chuck sleeve counterclockwise until the jaws open.

## 4. Operation

### Forward/Reverse Selector and Trigger Switch
WARNING: Accidental start-up hazard. Always verify that the forward/reverse selector is locked in the center off-position when the tool is not in use to prevent personal injury.

1. Rotational direction is controlled by the forward/reverse selector located above the trigger switch.
2. For forward rotation, push the forward/reverse selector to the left side of the tool.
3. For reverse rotation, push the forward/reverse selector to the right side of the tool.
4. Set the selector to the center position to lock the trigger switch.
5. The trigger switch controls operation and speed. Depress trigger to start; release completely to stop.
6. Spindle speed is variable. Light trigger pressure produces low RPM; increased pressure increases speed up to the selected gear maximum.

### Two-Speed Gearbox Adjustment
CAUTION: Gear damage hazard. Do not actuate the speed selector while the tool is running. Wait for the chuck to stop completely.

1. The two-speed gearbox provides two distinct operating ranges.
2. Slide speed selector forward to "1" for low speed (0-450 RPM) and maximum torque. Use for heavy-duty drilling or driving.
3. Slide speed selector backward to "2" for high speed (0-1,700 RPM). Use for fast drilling or small fasteners.
4. If selector resists shifting, briefly depress the trigger to rotate gears, then shift when the motor stops.

### Clutch and Torque Control
WARNING: Torque reaction hazard. Set the clutch to the appropriate level to match fastener size. Excessive torque can cause the tool to bind or twist, causing injury.

1. The 24-position clutch controls torque to prevent fastener damage.
2. Locate the clutch collar behind the chuck.
3. Rotate collar to align the desired number with the indicator arrow.
4. Settings 1 to 5 are for driving small screws in soft materials.
5. Settings 6 to 15 are for driving medium screws in standard materials.
6. Settings 16 to 23 are for driving larger screws or hard materials.
7. For drilling, align the drill mode icon with the indicator arrow to disable the clutch and deliver maximum torque (550 in-lbs).

### LED Work Light
1. The integrated LED work light is located on the handle base, above the battery pack slot.
2. The light activates automatically when the trigger is depressed slightly, before motor rotation begins, to illuminate the work area.
3. The light remains illuminated during use and turns off 10 seconds after the trigger is released.

### General Drilling Procedure
WARNING: Projectile and dust hazard. Always wear ANSI Z87.1 approved eye protection and a dust mask when performing drilling operations. Secure the workpiece in a vise or clamp to prevent rotation.

1. Insert the NF-B202 battery pack according to instructions in "Installing and Removing the Battery Pack".
2. Mark the center of the hole. If drilling in metal, use a center punch to prevent the drill bit from wandering.
3. Select and install the correct bit according to "Inserting and Removing Bits".
4. Rotate the clutch collar to the drill mode icon.
5. Slide the speed selector to low range "1" or high range "2" depending on material and bit diameter. Use low speed for hard materials and high speed for soft materials.
6. Place the tip of the drill bit directly on the marked target.
7. Hold the drill/driver firmly with both hands, maintaining perpendicular alignment with the workpiece.
8. Depress the trigger switch slowly to start the hole, then apply steady downward pressure. Do not force the tool or apply lateral force.
9. If the drill bit binds, release the trigger switch immediately. Change the forward/reverse selector to reverse and run slowly to back the bit out.
10. Reduce pressure as the bit is about to break through the back of the workpiece to prevent splintering or sudden binding.
11. Withdraw the bit straight out of the hole while the motor is still running.

### Screwdriving and Fastening Procedure
CAUTION: Fastener damage hazard. Ensure the screwdriver bit is fully seated in the screw head before operating to prevent damage to the bit, fastener, or workpiece.

1. Install a driving bit matching the screw head according to instructions in "Inserting and Removing Bits".
2. Adjust the clutch collar to a low setting (such as 3 or 5) to begin.
3. Select the low speed range "1" on the speed selector to maintain maximum control during the driving process.
4. Place the screw on the bit and position the screw tip against the workpiece.
5. Apply firm, steady downward force along the centerline of the screw to keep the driver bit engaged.
6. Depress the trigger switch slowly to start driving the screw.
7. Allow the clutch to slip (producing a clicking sound) once the selected torque limit is reached.
8. Release the trigger switch immediately when the clutch slips, then inspect the fastener depth. Increase the clutch setting if necessary.

## 5. Maintenance and Storage

### Cleaning and Lubrication
WARNING: Electrical shock and accidental start-up hazard. Always remove the NF-B202 battery pack from the drill/driver before performing any cleaning, maintenance, or inspection. Never immerse the tool, battery pack, or charger in water or any other liquid.

1. Wipe the outer plastic housing of the drill/driver with a clean, soft, damp cloth after each use. Remove any accumulated dirt or sawdust from the motor ventilation slots.
2. Use a soft, dry brush or low-pressure compressed air (maximum 30 PSI) to blow dust and debris out of the brushless motor vents and trigger switch assembly.
3. Clean the clutch collar and speed selector area with a small, dry brush to prevent dirt buildup from restricting movement.
4. Keep all tool handles and gripping surfaces dry and free from oil or grease.

CAUTION: Chemical damage hazard. Do not use gasoline, carbon tetrachloride, chlorinated cleaning solvents, or ammonia to clean the tool. These chemicals can degrade and weaken the plastic materials of the housing.

### Chuck and Accessory Maintenance
WARNING: Projectile hazard. Regularly inspect the 1/2-inch single-sleeve ratcheting chuck for signs of damage or wear. A damaged chuck can fail to grip bits securely during high-torque applications, leading to thrown accessories and potential injury.

1. Open the chuck jaws completely by rotating the single-sleeve ratcheting chuck counterclockwise.
2. Turn the tool upside down and tap the chuck gently to shake out any accumulated dust or wood debris from the jaw mechanism.
3. Spray dry PTFE lubricant directly into the chuck jaws. Do not use standard wet oils, as they attract dust and grit, which can seize the ratcheting mechanism.
4. Periodically verify that the internal chuck screw inside the chuck opening is tight. Use a manual screwdriver to turn this left-hand thread screw counterclockwise to tighten.
5. Inspect the belt hook and belt hook screw regularly. Ensure the screw is tight to prevent the drill/driver from falling. Refer to "Installing the Belt Hook".

### Battery Pack and Charger Care
WARNING: Battery damage and chemical exposure hazard. Never attempt to disassemble or modify the NF-B202 battery pack or the NF-C20 charger. There are no user-serviceable parts inside. If the battery pack housing is cracked or damaged, discontinue use immediately.

1. Inspect the metal electrical contacts inside the battery port of the tool, on the battery pack, and inside the charger slot before each use. Wipe contacts with a dry, clean cloth if they appear dusty or dirty.
2. Keep the charger ventilation slots clear of dust and debris to ensure proper heat dissipation during the charging cycle.
3. Clean the charger housing using only a dry, non-metallic brush or cloth. Always unplug the charger from the 120V AC outlet before cleaning.

### Storage Guidelines
WARNING: Fire and explosion hazard. Do not store the drill/driver with the battery pack installed. Store the tool, battery pack, and charger in a cool, dry, secure location out of the reach of children. Do not store near heaters, open flames, or where temperatures can exceed 122°F (50°C).

1. Remove the NF-B202 battery pack from the handle base before storing the tool. Place the forward/reverse selector in the locked center off-position.
2. Remove any drill bit or screwdriver bit from the 1/2-inch ratcheting chuck. Refer to "Inserting and Removing Bits" for instructions on proper removal.
3. Clean the tool and battery pack thoroughly according to the procedures listed in "Cleaning and Lubrication" before storage.
4. For long-term storage exceeding 30 days, ensure the battery pack is stored in an environment with temperatures between 32°F and 104°F (0°C to 40°C).
5. Discharge the battery pack to 50 percent capacity prior to storage to prevent cell degradation. Do not store a completely discharged battery pack.
6. Store the charger disconnected from the power source and separate from the battery pack.

### Storage Specifications
Storage temperature range: -4°F to 122°F (-20°C to 50°C)
Optimal battery storage capacity: 50%
Recommended storage humidity: Less than 80% RH
Maximum storage period (without recharging): 6 months

## 6. Troubleshooting

### Drill and Driver Subsystem

Problem: Drill/driver does not start when trigger switch is depressed.
Possible cause: Forward/reverse selector is in the locked center off-position; battery pack is not fully seated; battery pack is completely discharged.
Corrective action: Push the forward/reverse selector to the left or right directional position. Reinsert the NF-B202 battery pack until the latch clicks. Charge the battery pack using the NF-C20 charger.

Problem: Chuck slips or drill/driver fails to drive fasteners.
Possible cause: Clutch collar is set to a torque limit that is too low; speed selector is in the incorrect range; drill bit or driver bit is worn.
Corrective action: Rotate the clutch collar to a higher numerical setting. Slide the speed selector forward to low range "1" for high torque. Replace the worn bit.

Problem: Single-sleeve ratcheting chuck does not grip or release bits.
Possible cause: Dust or wood debris inside the chuck jaws; ratcheting mechanism is seized.
Corrective action: Open the chuck jaws completely. Turn the tool upside down and tap gently to clear debris. Spray dry PTFE lubricant into the chuck jaws.

Problem: Speed selector resists shifting or does not engage.
Possible cause: Gearbox teeth are misaligned; speed selector was shifted while the motor was running.
Corrective action: Release the trigger switch and wait for the tool to stop completely. Briefly depress the trigger switch to rotate the internal gears, then slide the speed selector to "1" or "2" once the motor stops.

Problem: LED work light does not illuminate.
Possible cause: Battery pack is depleted; internal LED electrical connection is faulty.
Corrective action: Charge the NF-B202 battery pack. If the battery is charged and the LED work light still fails to illuminate after depressing the trigger switch, contact an authorized service center.

### Battery and Charger Subsystem

Problem: Charger LED remains solid green when battery pack is inserted.
Possible cause: Battery pack is not fully seated; charger contacts or battery contacts are dirty.
Corrective action: Slide the NF-B202 battery pack firmly into the NF-C20 charger until it seats completely. Unplug the charger, clean the electrical contacts with a dry, clean cloth, and try again.

Problem: Charger LED indicates charging fault.
Possible cause: Battery pack is too hot or too cold; battery pack is damaged.
Corrective action: Remove the battery pack from the charger and allow it to reach optimal operating temperature before re-inserting. Discontinue use immediately if the battery pack housing is cracked or damaged.
